File Types

What To Send

The better the file, the sharper the finished product.

Best

Vector Files

Logos and text built from lines and shapes. They scale to any size with perfectly clean edges.

  • .AI
  • .EPS
  • .SVG
  • .PDF (vector)
Good

High-Res Photos

At least 300 dpi at the size it will be printed. Check the size before you send it, not after.

  • .PNG
  • .JPG
  • .TIFF
  • 300 dpi
Avoid

Low-Res & AI Images

Screenshots and images saved from websites or social media turn blurry and pixelated. AI-generated files generally don't meet production requirements.

  • Screenshots
  • Social media saves
  • AI images
